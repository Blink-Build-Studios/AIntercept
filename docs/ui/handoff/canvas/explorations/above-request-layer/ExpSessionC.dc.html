Rules
PartRule
PhaseA run of turns with no gap longer than 45 min, cut again at local midnight. Header: day/part of day, span, turns, the phase’s work line, effort.
GapsA thin divider names the gap: idle 1h 12m, overnight · 20h 20m: just the time between turns, no presence data.
SubagentsA spawned subagent appears under the turn that spawned it, as one ⤷ row with its own work and effort; open thread › opens it as its own session (Codex) or a filtered view (Claude Code sidechain).
Hundreds of turnsPhases open one at a time; inside a phase the ledger pages at 50 turns. A 400-turn, 5-day session is ~12 phase rows.
CodexSame structure; the work line has tool counts only (ran 14 commands · patched 2 files).
Open questionIs 45 min the right gap? Alternatives: cut only at days, or let the person’s own breaks define it (largest gaps first, max 12 phases).
Open questionA phase’s work line needs per-phase sums. Since phases are derived from turn times, can they be computed at read time from turn rows (cheap), or must they be stored?
